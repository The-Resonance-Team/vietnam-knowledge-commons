# Legal and licensing research for VNKC

**Scope:** Copyright status of Vietnamese legal/administrative documents, portal terms of use, database/data law, personal-data law, archiving, crawling practice, and a recommended licensing strategy for the Viet Nam Knowledge Commons (VNKC).

**Reading guide.** Each section separates three layers:

- **What the law says (cited)** — claims tied to a named instrument, article, primary URL, and confidence level (`verified` = read on a primary source on 2026-07-27; `partial` = primary source identified but text read via a mirror/secondary source, or fetch incomplete; `unverified` = not yet confirmed against a primary source).
- **Our risk assessment (opinion)** — project judgment, not legal advice.
- **Needs professional review** — questions reserved for qualified Vietnamese counsel.

**Default position until legal review is complete: reference-only.** We do not assert that any collected content may be redistributed; each source gets a license manifest recording the cited basis (or absence of one) before publication.

Access date for all URLs below: **2026-07-27**.

## 1. Copyright status of Vietnamese legal and administrative documents

### What the law says (cited)

**Instrument:** Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11 of 29 November 2005 (Luật Sở hữu trí tuệ), as amended by Laws 36/2009/QH12, 42/2019/QH14, 07/2022/QH15, and 131/2025/QH15.

**Article 15 — "Các đối tượng không thuộc phạm vi bảo hộ quyền tác giả"** (Subject matters not covered by copyright protection). Vietnamese text, clauses 1–3 (original 2005 text, unchanged by later amendments except as noted):

> **Điều 15. Các đối tượng không thuộc phạm vi bảo hộ quyền tác giả**
>
> 1. Tin tức thức sự thuần tuý đưa tin.
> 2. Văn bản quy phạm pháp luật, văn bản hành chính, văn bản khác thuộc lĩnh vực tư pháp và bản dịch chính thức của văn bản đó.
> 3. Quy trình, hệ thống, phương pháp hoạt động, khái niệm, nguyên lý, số liệu.

WIPO Lex English translation (official): "1. News of the day as mere items of press information. 2. Legal documents, administrative documents and other documents in the judicial domain and official translations of these documents. 3. Processes, systems, operation methods, concepts, principles and data."

**2025 amendment (important, already in force):** Law No. 131/2025/QH15 of 10 December 2025, "Luật sửa đổi, bổ sung một số điều của Luật Sở hữu trí tuệ", effective **1 April 2026**, adds clause 4 to Article 15 (Điều 1, point 7 of the amending law: "Bổ sung khoản 4 vào sau khoản 3 Điều 15"):

> "4. Ý tưởng; khẩu hiệu; tên tác phẩm một cách độc lập." (Ideas; slogans; standalone titles of works.)

The 2025 amending law did **not** modify clauses 1–3. Since 2026-07-27 the four-clause version of Article 15 is in force.

### What is NOT excluded (and therefore potentially protected)

Derived from the scope of Article 14 (protected works) read against Article 15 — **confidence: partial**, characterization our analysis, to be confirmed by counsel:

- **Commentary, annotations, analysis, explanatory material** about laws (e.g. "bình luận", "phân tích" articles on ministry or news sites) — ordinary copyright works.
- **Creative selection and arrangement of a collection** (a curated anthology, a thematic compilation with editorial structure) — protectable as a compilation/derivative work even when every component document is excluded. _A raw, non-creative bulk mirror of all documents is less likely to attract compilation protection, but this is a judgment call._
- **Website design, HTML/CSS, logos, photographs, infographics** on government portals — excluded categories cover the _documents_, not the site's creative expression.
- **Non-official translations** — only "bản dịch chính thức" (official translations) are excluded.
- **Forms with creative content** — see §2.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

The body text of a Vietnamese law, decree, circular, decision, or administrative document published on vbpl.vn / vanban.chinhphu.vn / congbao.chinhphu.vn is not a copyrighted work under Article 15.2. Storing, transforming, and re-publishing those texts carries low copyright risk **under the statute**. The residual risks are (a) contractual/attribution notices on portals (§3), (b) copying portal _presentation_ (site design, editorial additions), and (c) misclassification of content that is not in fact a "văn bản quy phạm pháp luật / văn bản hành chính" (guides, FAQs, news articles on the same sites).

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** Core legal/administrative document texts are excluded from copyright by Art. 15.2 (verified). Treat portal site design, editorial commentary, and curated compilations as protected; store document text + provenance, not portal page chrome.

## 2. Official government forms (biểu mẫu) and attachments

### What the law says (cited)

No instrument located that imposes restrictions on biểu mẫu _beyond_ the general copyright framework — **unverified absence** (not proof none exists).

Analysis anchored to Art. 15.2 (verified above): a form that is itself issued as, or as an integral appendix (phụ lục) of, a normative/administrative document (e.g. "Mẫu số 01 ban hành kèm theo Thông tư …") is part of that excluded document. A form with substantial creative/visual design published _outside_ an excluded document is not obviously covered by Art. 15.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

- Forms bound to an issuing instrument ("ban hành kèm theo [số văn bản]"): treat as excluded, record the parent instrument number in the manifest.
- Standalone templates from portal "thư viện biểu mẫu" sections: provenance must record the issuing instrument; if none, treat as potentially protected → reference-only.
- Attachments that are _not_ documents of the state (third-party annexes, scanned contracts, lists of individuals): do not assume exclusion; review per item. Personal-data rules (§5) often matter more than copyright here.

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** Forms attached to a normative instrument inherit that instrument's Art. 15.2 exclusion (reasoned from cited law; partial confidence). Standalone or creative forms default to reference-only until reviewed.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

- The pattern on Vietnamese government portals is a **copyright symbol + "ghi rõ nguồn … khi phát hành lại"** — i.e. republication is contemplated and conditioned on attribution, not prohibited. vanban.chinhphu.vn states this explicitly (verified). We did **not** find any portal demanding more than attribution for reuse of _information_, but absence of a captured notice ≠ absence of terms; several sites are JS-heavy or were unreachable.
- These notices are best read as contract/attribution conditions layered on top of the statutory position (§1). Honouring "ghi rõ nguồn" in per-source manifests and dataset metadata is cheap and covers the strictest demand we actually found.
- Two portals of interest have structurally changed since project planning: **vbpl.vn is now the National Legal Database under the Ministry of Justice**, and **the National Public Service Portal sits under the National Data Center, Ministry of Public Security**; **data.gov.vn no longer resolves**. Source manifests must record _current_ operator names.

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** Only vanban.chinhphu.vn/chinhphu.vn has a verified reuse notice, and it demands attribution only. Record "ghi rõ nguồn: Cục Thông tin và Truyền thông Chính phủ / www.chinhphu.vn" for all chinhphu.vn-family content; re-check each portal's terms page before first bulk publication (several were unreachable or JS-only today).

## 4. Database extraction, the Law on Data, and access-to-information law

### Sui generis database right

**What the law says (cited):** Vietnamese IP law protects copyright in "tác phẩm" (works) defined by creative originality (Law 50/2005/QH11, Art. 14 — partial confidence, scope read via WIPO Lex record). No provision creating an EU-style sui generis database right (protection of non-original compilations by investment) was located in the IP Law — **unverified absence**, to be confirmed by counsel. If confirmed: raw facts and non-creative compilations are free; only creative selection/arrangement is protected (see §1, "not excluded").

### Law on Data 2024

**What the law says (cited) — verified.** Law on Data No. 60/2024/QH15 (Luật Dữ liệu), passed 30 November 2024, effective **1 July 2025**. Full text read on the Government's policy portal (xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-luat-du-lieu-119250226145839949.htm; instrument also at thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Luat-Du-lieu-2024-so-60-2024-QH15-621343.aspx):

- **Điều 21 (Công khai dữ liệu):** data must be published faithfully to source and "thuận lợi cho tổ chức, cá nhân trong việc khai thác, sử dụng, chia sẻ" (convenient for exploitation, use, sharing); what may be published is governed by access-to-information law; state agencies "có trách nhiệm công bố danh mục dữ liệu mở và tổ chức công khai dữ liệu mở … để tổ chức, cá nhân khai thác, sử dụng, chia sẻ" (must publish open-data catalogues for organizations and individuals to exploit, use, share).
- **Điều 35.3(c):** organizations and individuals may "**tự do khai thác và sử dụng dữ liệu mở**" (freely exploit and use open data) from the National Integrated Database; personal data or other non-open data require consent of the National Data Center (and the data subject, for personal data).
- **Điều 38:** exploiting/using data in state-managed databases is free for state bodies and for data about yourself; others "phải nộp phí theo quy định của pháp luật về phí và lệ phí" (may owe statutory fees).

Implementing decrees detailing Điều 21/35/38 ("Chính phủ quy định chi tiết Điều này") were noted in the text but not individually verified — **needs verification**.

### Access to information

**What the law says (cited) — partial.** Law on Access to Information No. 104/2016/QH13 (Luật Tiếp cận thông tin; thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Luat-tiep-can-thong-tin-2016-280116.aspx) establishes citizens' right to access state information and agencies' proactive publication duties. **A replacement, Law on Access to Information 2026 No. 01/2026/QH16, was passed and takes effect 1 September 2026** (luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/luat-tiep-can-thong-tin-2026-so-01-2026-qh16-434736-d1.html; thuvienphapluat.vn policy note vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/chinh-sach-moi/112448). Provisions cited at article level not yet re-read against the 2026 text — article numbers below are therefore intentionally omitted.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

- The Law on Data is structurally **favourable** to VNKC: it creates a statutory concept of open data that the public may "freely exploit and use" (Điều 35.3(c)) and obliges agencies to publish open-data catalogues (Điều 21). This is the strongest _positive_ basis found for dataset redistribution — but it applies to "dữ liệu mở" as designated by the state, not to everything a portal happens to publish, and the fee clause (Điều 38) signals the state contemplates controlled exploitation channels.
- No sui generis database right found → the main residual database risk is _compilation copyright_ in creative arrangements (§1) plus contractual portal terms (§3).
- The Access-to-Information regime supports republication of proactively published state information in principle; the 2026 replacement (effective in ~5 weeks from research date) must be reviewed before launch because it may renumber or tighten provisions.

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** No database sui generis right located (unverified absence). Law on Data 60/2024/QH15 Điều 21 + 35.3(c) gives a positive statutory basis to reuse officially designated **open data**; Access to Information Law (2016, replaced by 01/2026/QH16 from 2026-09-01) supports access. Anchor dataset claims to "open data" designations where available; flag implementing decrees and the 2026 A2I law for counsel.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

- **In force today:** PDPL 91/2025/QH15 is the governing statute; Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP remains as implementing detail until superseded. Article-level mapping of PDPL not yet done — flag for counsel.
- Names, titles, and signatures of officials on published legal documents are personal data in a literal sense. However: (a) they are published by the state in an official capacity under access-to-information and law-promulgation regimes; (b) our purpose (documenting the _document_, not the person) is not profiling. Storing signatory names as document metadata is assessed **low risk**.
- **Higher-risk content** (do not bulk-collect without review): lists of individuals (beneficiaries, licensees, sanctioned persons, scholarship recipients), scanned IDs/certificates in attachments, CVs of officials, and any "dữ liệu cá nhân nhạy cảm" (sensitive data — health, biometrics, etc. per Decree 13 Điều 2.4). A searchable person-index built across documents would be a new "processing" purpose and needs a legal basis — keep VNKC document-centric.
- Practical mitigations: strip or hash personal identifiers in derived datasets unless document-meaningful; keep a takedown/correction contact; do not republish raw attachments containing ID numbers.

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** PDPL 91/2025/QH15 is in force (verified) — treat signatory names as incidental document metadata (low risk), prohibit bulk collection of person-lists/IDs in attachments, and get the PDPL article-level analysis done by counsel before publishing person-searchable features.

## 6. Storing historical snapshots / archiving government web content

### What the law says (cited)

No Vietnamese instrument specifically prohibiting private archiving of publicly accessible government web content was located — **unverified absence**. Law on Data Điều 14 (verified above) regulates data-storage _obligations of state agencies_ and owners of "core/important data", not third-party archiving of public pages. Copyright analysis of §1 applies to archived copies (document texts excluded; site design protected — an archive that reproduces full page chrome is riskier than one storing content + metadata).

### International practice (practice notes, not Vietnamese law)

- **Gesetze-im-Internet (Germany):** federal justice ministry publishes consolidated law texts relying on §5 UrhG (official works not protected); widely mirrored.
- **EveryPolitician (mySociety, archived 2019):** aggregated official parliamentary data with per-source licence notes — the per-source-manifest model VNKC proposes.
- **OpenGov Foundation / GovTrack (US):** rely on US government-works public-domain rule (17 U.S.C. §105) — a US-specific basis that does **not** transfer to Viet Nam; do not cite it as precedent for VNKC's legal position, only as architectural precedent.

### Our risk assessment (opinion)

Versioned snapshots of _document texts_ with provenance metadata align with the statutory exclusion (Art. 15.2) and with the Law on Data's publication-faithfulness requirement (Điều 21.1 — snapshots are evidence of fidelity). Main exposures: personal data frozen in old snapshots (§5), and later-repealed documents being misread as current — mitigate by surfacing validity status ("hiệu lực") prominently, as vbpl.vn itself does.

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** No archiving prohibition found (unverified absence). Archive document text + provenance + validity status, not full site presentation; apply §5 personal-data hygiene to snapshots.

## 7. robots.txt and technical access

### Observed (verified 2026-07-27)

```
# https://vbpl.vn/robots.txt
User-Agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/
Disallow: /Pages/
Sitemap: https://vbpl.vn/sitemap.xml

# https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/robots.txt
User-agent: *
Allow: /
```

Both permit general crawling; vbpl.vn excludes its `/api/` and `/Pages/` paths (legacy endpoints) and offers a sitemap — **partial** (two sites checked; others not yet).

### Our risk assessment (opinion; not legal advice)

- Vietnamese law contains no established anti-scraping offence applicable to respectful crawling of public pages that we could verify (unverified absence). Practical risks are: breach of posted terms (§3), overloading state infrastructure (courtesy/cybersecurity optics), and IP-based blocking.
- Documented practice for this project: identify the crawler (`User-Agent: VNKC-bot (+contact)`), honour robots.txt (note vbpl.vn's `/api/` exclusion — do not crawl internal APIs), keep request rates low (≤1 req/2s per host), fetch off-peak (ICT Viet Nam time), cache aggressively, and prefer official bulk/export channels where offered (sitemap, Cổng dữ liệu quốc gia open-data catalogue under Law on Data Điều 21).
- Distinguish _publication_ from _collection_: crawling for collection is near-universally tolerated when respectful; the legal weight sits on what we republish (§§1–5).

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** vbpl.vn and vanban.chinhphu.vn robots.txt allow crawling (verified) — crawl politely with a declared UA, respect the `/api/` disallow, prefer sitemaps/official open-data channels, and treat robots.txt as a floor, not a licence.

## 8. Recommended licensing strategy

### Why one blanket license cannot cover collected data

A license only binds where the licensor holds rights. VNKC holds **no rights** in the underlying document texts (nobody does — Art. 15.2 exclusion), no rights in portal-protected elements (design, commentary, creative compilations), and cannot "license" personal data or state-controlled non-open data. Asserting e.g. "CC0 over the whole dataset" would be legally void for components we don't own and misleading to reusers about encumbered components. What VNKC _can_ license is (a) its code and (b) its original contributions: schemas, identifiers, normalization rules, derived structure, summaries, benchmarks, documentation.

### Proposal (opinion, pending review)

| Layer                                                                                                           | Proposed terms                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Rationale / tradeoffs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

Publish content as _reference-only_ until each manifest is counsel-approved. | Avoids void blanket claims; preserves attribution duties actually found (§3); makes the evidentiary basis auditable per item.                                                                                                                                                                                               |

### Needs professional legal review (contract counsel in Viet Nam)

1. Confirm Art. 15.2 coverage of every document class VNKC targets (esp. "văn bản hành chính" vs "văn bản chuyên môn", party documents, pre-1945/historical documents).
2. Confirm absence of sui generis database right and assess compilation-copyright exposure of portal-sourced arrangements.
3. Review each portal's full terms (several unreachable/JS-only today) and whether "ghi rõ nguồn" notices create enforceable attribution conditions beyond statute.
4. Article-level analysis of PDPL 91/2025/QH15 (and surviving parts of Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP) for signatory names, attachments, cross-border hosting of the dataset.
5. Law on Data implementing decrees (Điều 21/35/38 detail): what counts as "dữ liệu mở", any fee/registration exposure for bulk exploitation.
6. Law on Access to Information 2026 (01/2026/QH16, effective 2026-09-01): renumbering/new limits affecting republication.
7. Cybersecurity/data-localization exposure of hosting Vietnamese state data snapshots abroad (not researched here).

> **Bottom line for VNKC:** Apache-2.0 for code; per-source manifests (not a blanket license) for collected documents, reference-only until review; CC-BY-4.0 for VNKC's original derived layers (CC0 fallback for pure metadata). All seven review items above gate public dataset launch.

## Unresolved questions for professional legal review

1. Exact boundary of "văn bản quy phạm pháp luật / văn bản hành chính / văn bản khác thuộc lĩnh vực tư pháp" (Art. 15.2) — which VNKC target classes fall inside (party documents, internal directives, historical/pre-1975 texts)?
2. Does any Vietnamese instrument create database/compilation protection beyond copyright in creative arrangement (confirm the sui generis absence)?
3. Are portal "ghi rõ nguồn" notices enforceable conditions of reuse, and against whom (contract vs notice only)?
4. PDPL 91/2025/QH15 article-level requirements for: incidental signatory data, bulk attachments containing personal data, dataset takedown/correction duties, cross-border transfer/hosting.
